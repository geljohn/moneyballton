Phase 1 Deliverable: Tactical Recruitment Profile – The "Mitoma Role"

Objective:
Define the exact tactical requirements for Brighton's left-wing function to inform data acquisition and feature engineering. This ensures the model identifies functional system fits rather than biological or statistical clones of Kaoru Mitoma.

1. The "Must-Haves" (Core Model Parameters)
These are the non-negotiable tactical traits. Players must exhibit high volume in these specific areas to execute the system's role successfully.

Progressive Ball Carrying (PrgC): The system relies on the winger receiving the ball out wide and driving it forward to relieve pressure and break defensive lines.

High-Volume 1v1 Take-Ons (Take-Ons Succ): The player must be a dynamic isolator capable of consistently beating their man on the outside or cutting inside.

Attacking Penalty Area Touches (Touches Att Pen): The replacement must penetrate the 18-yard box to generate tangible goal threats, avoiding touchline-hugging without an end product.

Press Resistance & Outlet Target (PrgR): The player will frequently receive the ball isolated against a fullback or amidst a double-team and must successfully receive progressive passes to move the team up the pitch.

2. The "Nice-to-Haves" (Development Runway)
These traits elevate a player to world-class status but are not required for a U23 rotational replacement on day one.

Elite Crossing: The model will accept slightly raw crossing execution, as the primary tactical mandate focuses on box entry and ball progression over traditional byline crossing.

Defensive Tracking: Offensive ball progression is heavily prioritized over defensive output (like tackles or blocks) for this specific attacking role.

3. The "Mitoma-Specifics" (Variables to Exclude)
These are highly individualized traits unique to Kaoru Mitoma that will be intentionally excluded from the model to avoid overfitting.

Foul Magnetism (Fls): High foul-drawing rates are a byproduct of specific kinetic techniques, not a core tactical necessity for advancing the ball.

Kinetic Deceleration: Unique pause-and-burst techniques cannot be quantified accurately through standard event data and will not be proxy-measured.
